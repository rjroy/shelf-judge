## Step 2 Phase 3 Transport Integration

The active-operation registry now binds its one claimed transport directly to the owning request's disconnect signal. A disconnect releases that transport, terminalizes the operation as `transport-lost`, and aborts the same registry-owned provider signal; the terminal record still prohibits replacement or reattachment.

The CLI SSE client accepts the initiating process's abort signal, passes it to the Bun Unix-socket fetch, preserves event identity across chunk boundaries, and cancels its response reader whenever streaming exits. The Next.js proxy passes `NextRequest.signal` into the server-side daemon request. The Node Unix-socket client explicitly destroys pre-response requests and established streamed responses on abort, and forwarded stream cancellation destroys the upstream socket while removing its signal listener. Tests prove each process-boundary handoff and the composable daemon transport-to-provider abort seam without inventing the Reflection and Analyst feature routes whose exact paths are deferred to their later implementation steps.

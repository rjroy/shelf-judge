# Expanded Profile Attention Opportunities

## Status, Authority, And Decision State

**Document status:** The owner approved this amendment on 2026-09-19. It is an approved, not-yet-implemented amendment: approval establishes its normative future behavior but does not itself change shipped Profile behavior.

**Settled current authority:** [Useful Collection Profile](../../reference/specs/current/useful-collection-profile.md) remains authoritative for shipped behavior and retained intention lifecycle rules: every active owner-maintained Want to play intention, including preserved historical `first-play` and `replay` intentions, renders as one `play-intention` card. Missing, invalid, unavailable, or stale play evidence attaches a warning but never hides that active card. Ownership transition to previously owned retires an active intention; re-owning does not recreate one. Completion, retirement, baseline handling, conflict/replay behavior, and history remain governed by that authority until implementation reconciles this amendment with the reference and evidence.

**Approved amendment:** REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-1 through REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-19 are approved requirements for three initially requested opportunity families: derived `unplayed-owner-wanted`, `incomplete-rating`, and `purchase-utilization-goal`. Their owner-facing behavior, actions, resolution, and evidence expectations are defined below as approved future behavior. `unplayed-owner-wanted` is not a new durable intention kind, does not create a second card, and does not replace or reinterpret legacy intentions.

**Still unavailable:** rating, utilization, suppression, and AI capabilities remain unavailable until their separately named approvals or contracts and implementation. In particular, `incomplete-rating` and `purchase-utilization-goal` require approval and implementation of their named underlying data contracts before they can qualify, render, migrate, or be enabled.

**Deferred beyond this amendment:** no other family is approved. The rating-workflow and purchase-utilization-goal data contracts are explicit approval and implementation prerequisites, not a substitute for the approved family behavior below.

## Goal And Posture

The Profile continues to answer exactly two questions:

1. **What does my collection reveal about me?**
2. **What deserves my attention or a decision now?**

The second answer is a small, owner-controlled inbox. It may truthfully say **Nothing needs attention right now.** It must not use aging, due dates, reminders, streaks, neglected-game language, guilt, loss framing, queue size, or time-based ranking. Leaving an item visible is a valid response.

## Family, Compatibility, And Rendering Contract

### Preserved `play-intention` Compatibility Family

`play-intention` is an explicit preserved family, not an undeclared fourth family. It is governed by Useful Collection Profile's current Attention Item Contract and intention lifecycle. It covers every active intention that does not satisfy the narrower approved `unplayed-owner-wanted` predicate, including:

- active replay intentions and Want to play intentions with a valid nonzero baseline or current count;
- active general Want to play intentions created without a count baseline;
- active legacy first-play/replay intentions with absent, invalid, unavailable, conflicting, or stale current play evidence; and
- any active intention while current evidence cannot authorize automatic completion.

Its stable identity remains the durable `intentionId`; its card ID derives from that ID; its question remains the established owner-language play/replay question; and it keeps the existing evidence warning, correction, completion, retirement, manual-completion, ownership-transition, history, and no-reopen behavior. A profile read never mutates it.

### Approved `unplayed-owner-wanted` Presentation Family

For a single active intention, the daemon selects exactly one presentation family. Replay intentions always select `play-intention`, including when their current trustworthy count is exactly zero: replay is a compatibility play-intention whose owner relevance is the expressed wish to replay, not an inference that it is unplayed. A non-replay active Want to play intention selects `unplayed-owner-wanted` only when the game is currently owned and that same intention has a trustworthy current play count of exactly zero. Otherwise it selects `play-intention`. Both presentations retain the same `intentionId`, card identity, underlying lifecycle, actions, and history. A later evidence change may change an eligible non-replay presentation back to `play-intention`; it is not a resolution, migration, or new card.

Migration is therefore projection-only: existing durable intentions and resolved history are untouched, all active intentions first remain renderable through `play-intention`, and a recomputation may present an eligible existing intention as `unplayed-owner-wanted` without changing its durable kind, baseline, version, or resolution. No intention may produce two cards.

### Approved Rating And Utilization Families, Gated By Data-Contract Approval

| Family | Approved owner-facing behavior | Required approved data contract before implementation |

The owner may fill the fields or explicitly choose **Complete as is** if the approved contract permits it. The existing purchase-utilization specification is calculation and game-detail authority, not a goal contract. Until both this amendment and the respective data contract are approved and implemented, the rating and utilization families produce no cards or source-state changes.

## Qualification And Evidence

Every reported card must have: (1) explicit owner relevance, (2) a concrete useful action with a destination, and (3) named current evidence explaining why it applies. Otherwise it abstains. Low plays, ownership, age, cost, value, discrepancy, score, BGG metadata, sort position, or a statistic alone never qualifies a card.

### Current Evidence Policy

For `unplayed-owner-wanted`, current play evidence is acceptable only when the current Useful Collection Profile evidence contract considers its non-negative count valid, identifies its source and observation time, and no later known collection refresh/check makes that observation stale. The count must be exactly zero. The exact-zero rule applies only after replay precedence: a replay intention remains `play-intention` even with this witness. If two accepted sources disagree, if a newer accepted check marks the count missing or invalid, or if freshness cannot be established, the zero-play predicate is false. The daemon must not select the approved family, infer that the owner still has zero plays, or automatically resolve the intention.

This is deterministic: select `unplayed-owner-wanted` only for a non-replay intention on a valid, current, unconflicted exact-zero witness; otherwise retain the active `play-intention` compatibility card with the exact current warning required by Useful Collection Profile. The current authority's observed-increase rule remains the only automatic completion rule. Evidence refresh/correction is an owner action, not a read-side mutation. The approved rating and goal contracts must supply equally deterministic current, conflict-free witnesses before their approved predicates can be evaluated.

Each card exposes its family, stable game and underlying-state identity, plain-language question, why-now explanation, owner-state creation/update time, evidence source/value/observation time when known, warning or conflict state, applicable actions, and authoritative destination. Future rating and goal contracts must define equivalent deterministic freshness and conflict policies before their families can be enabled; their evidence must not be treated as current by analogy.

## Timing, Resolution, And Suppression

There are no due dates, reminder schedules, age thresholds, urgency scores, overdue states, or time-based ranking. Cards order by NFC-normalized game name in Unicode code-point order, stable game ID, then presentation family ID. This is neutral ordering, not priority.

For `play-intention` and `unplayed-owner-wanted`, completion and retirement are the underlying intention's existing durable transitions. Recomputing, changing presentation family, losing evidence, or becoming not-qualified cannot fabricate resolution or reopen a resolved intention. A valid trusted later play witness whose count is greater than a trustworthy captured baseline completes the active intention during that evidence data update, as Useful Collection Profile requires; this is an observed-play completion, not a presentation-side correction. A new card after resolution requires a new explicit intention and new intention ID. The approved rating and goal family resolutions are the explicit durable transitions stated in their table rows and remain unavailable until their respective approved contracts define and authorize those transitions.

**Exploratory proposal:** a reversible, visible family-and-game suppression may later be added with actor, time, scope, and optional reason. It cannot delete evidence or source state, and it is not a snooze. Its persistence and interaction with compatibility cards require an explicit approved contract and implementation before a suppression action can be offered or enforced.

## Feedback

Every active card, including every active approved-family card and preserved compatibility card, must display **Was this worth bringing up?** with `Yes`, `No`, and `Skip`; it is not optional UI. Selecting an answer records a durable, owner-local feedback event containing the card's stable card ID, family, game ID, answer, and recorded-at time. It does not resolve, hide, or otherwise alter the card.

- `Yes` records `yes` and leaves the card visible and eligible under its normal rules.
- `Skip` records `skip` and leaves the card visible and eligible under its normal rules.
- `No` records `no`, leaves the card visible and eligible, and then offers, without a prompt loop or pressure, an optional reason. When the separately approved suppression contract has also been implemented, it additionally offers the optional visible family-and-game suppression action. Otherwise, no suppression action is offered; recording `no` and declining or supplying the optional reason is a complete No response.

Reasons are optional fixed owner-visible categories plus an optional short free-text note. They persist only as feedback attached to that event, are visible to and deletable by the owner, and are never a model instruction, prompt content, qualification input, ranking input, suppression inference, penalty, age signal, or urgency signal. Feedback events, their required metadata, and their reasons are local durable owner data, are retained until the owner deletes them, and must never be transmitted to a provider, aggregated, or shared absent a separately approved contract. An explicit approved suppression, if added, is a separate durable owner action with its own visible scope and reversal; feedback alone never creates it.

## LLM-Managed Settings: Bounded Future Proposal

Models are not needed for qualification, ordering, evidence validation, resolution projection, or suppression enforcement. Those remain deterministic daemon-owned behavior.

If separately approved, an optional assistant may only draft or apply a requested allowlisted setting change: enable/disable an eligible approved non-compatibility family, create/edit/retire an owner-defined goal only from an explicit owner request once a goal contract exists, or create/remove a visible suppression. Eligible families exclude preserved `play-intention` and its `unplayed-owner-wanted` presentation, so the assistant cannot disable, enable, or otherwise alter their Useful Collection Profile lifecycle or rendering behavior. It cannot infer a goal, create an unsolicited goal, create a Want to play intention, alter ratings or evidence, resolve an opportunity, recommend purchase/sale/play, configure credentials, broaden data access, or create timers.

### Pre-transmission Authorization

Before sending **any** owner data to a provider, the daemon must show the owner a pre-transmission authorization screen containing the resolved provider and exact model, the requested operation, and the exact evidence manifest. The manifest must enumerate every evidence category and each included item or field (for example, selected setting state, named game IDs/titles, intention metadata, play evidence, goal state, or suppression state), explicitly state that owner-note text is excluded, and identify any category with zero transmitted items. The owner must explicitly approve this exact provider/model and manifest after it is resolved. Approval is scoped to that one request and expires on provider, model, operation, or manifest change.

Before transmission, refusal, cancellation, closing the authorization, provider-resolution or validation failure, unavailable provider, or a changed authorization tuple (provider, model, operation, or manifest) sends no owner data and leaves settings, attention, durable state, and command receipts unchanged. No provider discovery, model fallback, prompt construction containing owner data, tool call, or transmission may occur before approval. After approved transmission, a provider failure or invalid output causes no state change and no additional owner-data transmission; it does not undo or misrepresent the earlier approved request as unsent.

After approval, the daemon alone uses the single pi-agent boundary with explicit operator provider/model configuration, allowlisted provider extensions and model-visible tools, bound-session capability inspection, strict input/output validation, redacted logs, and no direct web, CLI, route, or feature-service provider calls. Before applying an accepted setting change, show exact before/after state, evidence scope, and effect; validate and execute it as a normal daemon command; make it visible and reversible through non-model web and CLI controls. Owner preferences outrank model output.

## Requirements

1. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-1:** The Profile retains exactly two top-level questions: **What does my collection reveal about me?** and **What deserves my attention or a decision now?** It retains a successful **Nothing needs attention right now** state when no active card qualifies.
2. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-2:** A newly qualifying card must independently identify all three facts: (a) **owner relevance**, meaning an active owner-maintained intention, rating, or goal makes the item personally relevant; (b) a **useful action**, meaning a specific owner action and the screen or command where that action can be taken; and (c) **current evidence**, meaning named evidence with its source, value, and observation time that the **Useful Collection Profile** play-evidence contract, approved rating-workflow contract, or approved purchase-utilization-goal contract, respectively, considers valid, fresh, and unconflicted. If any fact is absent, the system must not newly qualify a card. An already active `play-intention` remains visible with the warning required by Useful Collection Profile when play evidence is missing, invalid, unavailable, stale, superseded, or conflicting. An active `unplayed-owner-wanted` card that loses its selection predicate must immediately be presented as its existing `play-intention` card, rather than removed, provided its underlying intention remains active. In contrast, an active `incomplete-rating` or `purchase-utilization-goal` card must cease to qualify and render when its required contract witness becomes missing, invalid, stale, superseded, or conflicting; it must not be preserved by the play-intention exception.
3. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-3:** Under the current **Useful Collection Profile** Attention Item Contract, `play-intention` remains the preserved compatibility card for every active Want to play intention unless this requirement selects `unplayed-owner-wanted`. For each active intention, select exactly one card: a replay intention always selects `play-intention` before evaluating its play count, even when valid current evidence says exactly zero plays; a non-replay Want to play intention selects `unplayed-owner-wanted` only when REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-5's predicate is true; all other active intentions select `play-intention`. Missing, unavailable, invalid, stale, superseded, or conflicting play evidence therefore selects the visible `play-intention` fallback with the warning required by Useful Collection Profile, rather than hiding the intention, inferring zero plays, or resolving it.
4. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-4:** The preserved `play-intention` card retains every behavior that **Useful Collection Profile** assigns to an active intention: its durable `intentionId` and derived card identity; established play or replay question; evidence warning and correction path; completion and manual-completion rules; retirement and ownership-transition handling; recorded history; and no-reopen rule after retirement or resolution. Reading the Profile, recomputing its cards, or rendering a card must not write, resolve, retire, reopen, correct, or otherwise mutate any durable intention, evidence, or history.
5. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-5:** `unplayed-owner-wanted` is only a different presentation of one existing active **non-replay** Want to play intention. It may be selected only while that game is currently owned and the intention has play-count evidence that the current Useful Collection Profile evidence contract considers valid, fresh, and unconflicted and whose count is exactly zero. Selecting it creates no durable intention kind or data, does not migrate or resolve the intention, and cannot create a second card: the same `intentionId`, lifecycle, actions, and history remain behind the one card.
6. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-6:** Introducing this amendment must preserve every existing intention's durable `intentionId`, durable kind, count baseline, version, resolution state, and history. Before a qualifying recomputation, existing active intentions remain visible as `play-intention`; a later deterministic recomputation may change only the card's displayed family between `play-intention` and `unplayed-owner-wanted` when REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-5's current-evidence predicate changes. The question and why-now presentation may consequently change, but no durable data changes and each active intention still renders exactly one card.
7. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-7:** For the exact-zero test in REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-5, a play witness is unusable if it is missing, invalid, stale, superseded by a later refresh or check, or contradictory with another accepted source. In every such case, the daemon must treat the exact-zero predicate as false and display the active `play-intention` compatibility card with the specific current-evidence warning required by **Useful Collection Profile**. It must not display `unplayed-owner-wanted` or claim that the owner has zero plays. Supersession alone is not a universal no-resolution rule: when a valid, trusted later witness has a nonzero count strictly greater than the intention's trustworthy captured baseline, Useful Collection Profile's observed-play rule completes the active intention during that evidence update. Only zero evidence that is inappropriate, missing, invalid, stale, superseded, or conflicting falls back to the visible play-intention warning without automatic completion.
8. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-8:** Every rendered card must visibly provide: its family; stable game ID and underlying intention, rating, or goal ID; the owner-facing question; a plain-language why-now explanation; the relevant owner-state creation or update time; evidence source, value, and observation time when known; a clear missing, stale, invalid, or conflict warning when applicable; every available owner action; and the authoritative screen or command destination for each action.
9. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-9:** `incomplete-rating` depends on a separately approved **rating-workflow contract** that defines started ratings, required and unset fields, valid/fresh/unconflicted witnesses, Complete-as-is availability, durable completion and reopen transitions, existing-data treatment, and the rating-editor destination. Until both that contract and this amendment are approved and implemented, this family must not qualify, render, create, migrate, or mutate any rating or other data. After approval, it qualifies only for a currently owned game with an owner-created rating in the contract's `started` state, one or more contract-required fields unset, and a current unconflicted rating witness; it must explain those fields and evidence, link to the rating editor, and resolve only through the contract-authorized completion or Complete-as-is transition. It may reopen only when that contract explicitly authorizes an eligible started state.
10. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-10:** `purchase-utilization-goal` depends on a separately approved **purchase-utilization-goal contract** that defines owner-selected targets, goal creation and lifecycle, ownership transitions, valid/fresh/unconflicted utilization witnesses, target evaluation, manual-met, durable resolution and reopen transitions, its relation to the purchase-utilization result, and goal editor/detail destinations. Until both that contract and this amendment are approved and implemented, this family must not qualify, render, create, migrate, or mutate a goal or other data. After approval, it qualifies only for a currently owned game with an owner-created active goal whose current unconflicted utilization witness shows that the owner-selected target is unmet; it must explain the goal, target, and current result, link to goal detail or editor, and resolve only through the contract-authorized met, manual-met, or retirement transition. It may reopen only when that contract explicitly authorizes a new or reopened eligible active goal.
11. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-11:** Cards must not use due dates, reminders, reminder schedules, notifications, age thresholds, snoozes, recurrence, urgency or overdue states, streaks, queue size, neglected-game language, guilt, loss framing, failure framing, or time-based ranking. Their only order is NFC-normalized game name in Unicode code-point order, then stable game ID, then presentation family ID; this ordering is neutral and does not indicate priority.
12. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-12:** **Useful Collection Profile** remains the authority for an intention's lifecycle, completion, retirement, ownership transition, and no-reopen behavior. A card-family presentation change, evidence loss, or failed qualification must not resolve, retire, reactivate, or reopen an intention. A valid trusted later play witness with a count strictly greater than a trustworthy captured baseline must instead complete the active intention during the evidence data update, as Useful Collection Profile requires; this authority-required observed-play completion is permitted even when the later evidence corrects or supersedes an earlier witness. After an intention is resolved or retired, a new active card requires a new explicit owner intention with a new `intentionId`, as Useful Collection Profile requires.
13. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-13:** Every active card must visibly offer **Yes**, **No**, and **Skip** feedback. Each selection persists a durable owner-local feedback event with stable card ID, family, game ID, answer (`yes`, `no`, or `skip`), and recorded-at time. Yes and Skip leave the card visible and normally eligible. No leaves the card visible and normally eligible and offers an optional reason without a prompt loop or pressure. No feedback answer may change the underlying intention, rating, goal, setting, evidence, ownership, eligibility, resolution, ranking, age, or urgency. Feedback events, their metadata, and reasons must not be transmitted to a provider, aggregated, or shared absent a separately approved contract. Only if a separately approved and implemented suppression contract exists may No additionally offer an optional explicit visible family-and-game suppression action; otherwise declining or supplying the optional reason completes No, and feedback alone never suppresses a card.
14. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-14:** The daemon, not a web or CLI client, must deterministically decide whether each card qualifies, whether its witness is fresh or conflicting, which family it presents as, its neutral order, its lifecycle projection, and whether an approved suppression applies. Web and CLI clients may render those daemon decisions and relay validated owner commands, but may not independently infer, qualify, reorder, resolve, suppress, or alter a card.
15. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-15:** An AI settings assistant is optional and unavailable unless separately approved. If approved, it may only perform an explicit owner-requested allowlisted operation: enable or disable an eligible approved non-compatibility family; create, edit, or retire an owner-defined goal only after the purchase-utilization-goal contract exists; or create or remove a visible suppression after its contract exists. Eligible family means an approved family other than preserved `play-intention` and its `unplayed-owner-wanted` presentation. The assistant must not disable, enable, or otherwise alter the preserved play-intention lifecycle or rendering behavior; infer or create an unsolicited goal; create a Want to play intention; alter a rating or evidence; resolve an opportunity; recommend buying, selling, keeping, avoiding, or playing a game; configure credentials; broaden data access; or create timers. Before application, it must show the exact before and after state, evidence scope, and effect. The daemon alone must use the single pi-agent boundary with explicitly configured provider/model, allowlisted provider extensions and model-visible tools, bound-session capability inspection, strict input/output validation, and redacted logs. Accepted changes must execute as normal daemon commands and remain visible and reversible through non-model web and CLI controls; owner preferences outrank model output.
16. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-16:** Before transmitting any owner data for the optional AI settings assistant, the daemon must first resolve and show a pre-transmission authorization screen with the exact provider, exact model, requested operation, and exact evidence manifest. The manifest must list every evidence category and every included item or field, state that owner-note text is excluded, and identify categories with zero transmitted items. The owner must explicitly approve that exact resolved request; approval is single-request only and becomes invalid before transmission if the provider, model, operation, or any manifest item changes. Before approval and transmission, refusal, cancellation, closing the screen, provider-resolution failure, validation failure, provider unavailability, or an invalid or changed authorization tuple (provider, model, operation, or manifest) must send no owner data and must not mutate settings, attention, durable state, or command receipts. Before approval, the daemon must not discover a provider, select a fallback model, construct a prompt containing owner data, invoke a provider or model-visible tool, or transmit data. After an approved request has been transmitted, a provider failure or invalid output must cause no state change and no additional owner-data transmission, but must not claim that the earlier approved request was not sent.
17. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-17:** The optional AI settings assistant must never transmit unapproved private text or data channels, including owner-game-note text, feedback-reason categories or free text, owner-entered messages, hidden UI fields, local logs, command history, credentials, or any data not itemized in the approved manifest required by REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-16. A future exception for a private channel requires a separately approved contract that makes the exact fields and owner-visible evidence manifest explicit and defines consent, privacy and retention, citations, freshness/conflict treatment, redaction, failure behavior, and deletion controls; the exception remains prohibited until that contract is implemented.
18. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-18:** This feature must not recommend buying, selling, keeping, avoiding, or playing any game. No card may qualify solely because a game has a low play count, high cost, low calculated value, an unfavorable score or discrepancy, old age, a queue or sort position, BGG metadata, a model output, or feedback. Those facts may appear only as evidence for a family whose independently satisfied owner relevance, action, and current-evidence predicate is defined by this amendment or its named approved contract.
19. **REQ-PROFILE-ATTN-19:** Feedback events retain stable card ID, family, game ID, answer, recorded-at time, and any fixed-category or optional free-text reason as durable owner-local feedback until the owner deletes them. The owner can view and delete the event and reason. Neither feedback events, metadata, nor reasons may be transmitted to a provider, aggregated, or shared absent a separately approved contract, and none may be used as model instructions or as inputs to qualification, ranking, suppression inference, penalties, age, or urgency.

## Out Of Scope And Explicit Deferrals

- Application implementation, migration, routes, UI, model calls, or settings changes are outside this documentation amendment and remain unimplemented.
- Enabling or rendering a approved rating or goal family before both this amendment and its named data contract are approved, including inferred rating incompleteness or automatic purchase goals.
- Any family beyond the preserved compatibility family and the three approved families.
- Owner-note-derived attention or model input without a separate contract.
- Reminders, schedules, recurrence, notification, or autonomous action.
- Global suppression and all unapproved model data access.
